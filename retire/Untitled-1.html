About Dad

For many years, Mr. Stanley Njoroge Mburu devoted his life to educating young minds, inspiring excellence, and serving with integrity.

His classroom was more than a place of learning; it was a place where dreams were nurtured, confidence was built, and futures were shaped.

Through his wisdom, patience and unwavering commitment, he became not only a respected teacher but also a mentor, guide and role model.
